## Subsetting
Files are the Google Fonts `vietnamese`, `latin-ext` and `latin` subsets.
They are variable fonts (Source Serif 4: wght 200-900, JetBrains Mono:
wght 400-800) served as static instances at 400 (normal/italic) and
700 (bold/italic). The browser fetches only the subsets a page needs.
